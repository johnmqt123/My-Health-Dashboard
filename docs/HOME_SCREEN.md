# Home Screen

## Purpose

The Home screen is the starting point of My Assistant.

Its purpose is to quickly answer the user's most important daily questions without overwhelming them.

The Home screen should be usable in less than 20 seconds.

---

## Layout

1. Greeting
2. Today's Focus
3. Quick Access
4. Assistant Message

---

## Greeting

Example:

Good Morning, John

Display the current date beneath the greeting.

---

## Today's Focus

Displays only today's important information.

Examples:

- Morning medications
- Weight
- Today's appointments
- Nutrition progress

Each item opens its full section when selected.

---

## Quick Access

User-configurable shortcuts.

Examples:

- Weather
- Emergency Packet
- Financial Spreadsheet
- Calendar
- Grocery List

The user chooses what appears here.

---

## Assistant Message

One brief observation.

Examples:

"You met your protein goal yesterday."

or

"Your next appointment is Thursday at 2:00 PM."

Display only one or two messages at a time.

---

## Design Goals

- No clutter
- No scrolling whenever possible
- Large touch targets
- Fast loading
- Personalized
- Calm appearance
## Today's Focus

Today's Focus displays the most important information for the current day.

The goal is to answer the question:

"What should I pay attention to today?"

The information displayed should change throughout the day as tasks are completed.

Examples:

Morning
- Morning medications
- Weigh yourself
- Today's appointments

Afternoon
- Exercise
- Nutrition progress

Evening
- Evening medications
- Prepare for tomorrow

Completed items should remain visible but be clearly marked as completed.

The number of items displayed should normally be limited to four or five to avoid clutter.
