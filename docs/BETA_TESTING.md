# Beta Testing & User Onboarding

## Beta Tester Invitation

Would you like to help me test an app I'm building?

It's a simple personal health organizer that helps you keep track of things like medications, blood pressure, weight, exercise, food, and other health information — all in one place.

The goal is to make it easier to remember what you've taken and done, see your health information over time, and have useful information available when you need it.

I'm looking for a few people to try it and tell me what makes sense, what's confusing, and what could be easier to use.

You don't need to be good with technology. In fact, that's one reason I'd like you to try it.

I'll help you get started, and you can stop anytime.

Would you be interested?

---

## Quick Start

Once you have access to the app:

1. Open the app.
2. Start with **Medications** and add the medications you take.
3. Add anything else you'd like to keep track of.
4. Use the app normally for a few days.
5. Tell me whenever something is confusing, doesn't work the way you expected, or you think could be easier.

**There are no wrong answers. You're testing the app — not yourself!**

---

## What I'm Looking For

Please tell me about anything that:

- Is confusing
- Is difficult to find
- Takes too many steps
- Doesn't work as expected
- Is difficult to read or understand
- You think could be made easier

Also tell me what you like and what you find useful.

**Don't worry about hurting my feelings. If something doesn't make sense, I need to know. That's exactly what beta testing is for.**
