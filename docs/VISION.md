# Vision

My Health Dashboard is intended to become my personal health assistant.

The long-term vision is to provide one place to manage:

- Medications
- Weight
- Exercise
- Blood Pressure
- Nutrition
- CPAP information
- Appointments
- Shopping lists
- Calendar
- Reports
- Personal reminders

The application should minimize typing, automate repetitive tasks, and make daily health tracking quick and enjoyable.

Future AI features may include:

- Meal analysis
- Medication suggestions
- Trend analysis
- Voice input
- Image recognition
